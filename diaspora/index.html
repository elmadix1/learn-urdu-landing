Questions

Before you decide

Who is this specialization really for?

For diaspora Pakistanis who already speak Urdu, fluently or nearly so, but feel it stopped growing at the family level. If you can hold a conversation but struggle with ideas, culture and depth, this is built for you. It takes your inherited Urdu and makes it a full, adult command of the language.

I can't read or write the script yet. Can I start here?

Not with this course alone. This is an advanced specialization that assumes you can already read Urdu. If you cannot yet read the script, begin with our foundation course (A1 to B1), which teaches reading and writing from zero. The easiest path is the bundle below: it includes the full foundation, then this specialization, taking you all the way from the alphabet to mastery.

My Urdu is fluent but only about everyday things. Is that a problem?

That is exactly the starting point this course is designed for. Most of the diaspora speaks a warm but limited Urdu: family, food, feelings. This specialization fills the gaps your family never covered (ideas, opinions, culture) and lifts you to a complete adult command.

How is this different from your Poetry course?

The Poetry specialization studies Urdu literature for its own sake: form, technique, the great poets as art. This Diaspora course touches poetry too, but as a way home: understanding the verses your elders carried, reconnecting with your roots. The angle is identity, not literary study.

How long will it take?

At 3 to 4 hours per week, expect to reach a confident, cultured command in about 6 months. Because you already speak, progress is faster than you think. Every lesson builds on Urdu you already carry.

Is access really for life?

Yes. One payment, lifetime access, including all future updates. No subscriptions.
