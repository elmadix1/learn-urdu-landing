Why Urdu for your child?

A language is a gift that lasts a lifetime. Here's what your child gains.

Talk to grandparents

Real conversations with nana, nani, dada, dadi. The connection that only their own language can create.

Read and write

The beautiful Nastaliq script, learned step by step, in a way that feels like play, not homework.

Keep their roots

Growing up abroad doesn't mean losing the heritage. Urdu keeps your child connected to who they are.

A sharper mind

Bilingual children develop stronger memory, focus, and problem-solving. A second language is a gift for life.

Parents' questions

Everything you might want to know.

What age is this for?

Lessons are designed for children up to 12 years old, boys and girls. For older girls and women, see our Urdu for Women lessons, also with Maryam.

My child knows no Urdu at all. Is that okay?

Absolutely. Maryam starts wherever your child is, even from the very first letter. Total beginners are welcome.

How long is each lesson?

Each lesson is 50 minutes, one-on-one. There's also a shorter 30-minute trial lesson so your child can meet Maryam first.

How much does it cost?

Your first trial lesson is at a special introductory price so you can try with no big commitment. Full pricing is shown when you book.

What do we need?

Just a device with Zoom (phone, tablet, or computer) and a quiet space. Maryam provides all the learning materials.

Can I be there during the lesson?

Yes, and you're encouraged to, especially at the beginning. Many parents enjoy learning alongside their child.
