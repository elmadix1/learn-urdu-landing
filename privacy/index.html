Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how UrduAcademy collects, uses, and protects your personal data when you visit our website at learn-urdu.online, use our learning platform at class.learn-urdu.online, or purchase a course. We are committed to handling your data responsibly and transparently.

The data controller is XMathpro. For any privacy question, contact us at contact@learn-urdu.online.

1. The data we collect

We collect the following categories of data:

  • Account data: your name and email address when you create an account or enrol in a course.
  • Learning data: your course progress, quiz results, and activity on the learning platform.
  • Payment data: handled by Paddle, our payment processor. We do not store your full card details. We receive confirmation of your purchase and limited billing information from Paddle.
  • Technical data: such as your IP address, browser type, device, and pages visited, collected automatically to operate and improve the Service.
  • Communications: the content of any emails or messages you send us.

2. How we use your data

We use your data to:

  • provide access to the courses you purchase and track your progress;
  • process your purchase through Paddle and deliver your order;
  • respond to your questions and provide support;
  • send you important information about your account or courses;
  • send you marketing emails about our courses, only where you have agreed to receive them, with the ability to unsubscribe at any time;
  • maintain the security of the Service and improve our content.

3. Payment processing and Paddle

Payments are processed by Paddle.com, which acts as the Merchant of Record for our sales. When you make a purchase, your payment information is provided directly to Paddle and is handled under Paddle's own privacy policy. We encourage you to review it on the Paddle website. We do not receive or store your full payment card number.

4. Legal bases for processing

Where applicable data protection law (such as the GDPR) applies, we process your data on the following bases: to perform our contract with you (providing the courses you bought), for our legitimate interests (improving and securing the Service), to comply with legal obligations (such as tax and accounting), and with your consent (for marketing emails).

5. Sharing your data

We share your data only as needed to operate the Service, including with:

  • Paddle, for payment processing and tax handling;
  • our hosting and learning-platform providers, who store data on our behalf;
  • email and analytics providers that help us communicate with you and understand site usage;
  • authorities, where required by law.

We do not sell your personal data to anyone.

6. Cookies and analytics

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to make the site work, remember your preferences, and understand how the site is used. You can control cookies through your browser settings. Some features may not work properly if you disable cookies.

7. Data retention

We keep your account and learning data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you with course access. We keep transaction records for as long as required by law (for example, for tax and accounting). When data is no longer needed, we delete or anonymise it.

8. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access the personal data we hold about you, to correct it, to request its deletion, to object to or restrict certain processing, to withdraw consent, and to receive a copy of your data in a portable format. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at contact@learn-urdu.online. You may also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

9. Data security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data against loss, misuse, and unauthorised access. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. International transfers

Your data may be processed in countries other than your own, including by our service providers. Where this happens, we take steps to ensure your data continues to be protected in line with applicable law.

11. Children's privacy

The Service is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children without appropriate parental consent. If you believe a child has provided us data without such consent, please contact us and we will take appropriate action.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

13. Contact

For any question about this Privacy Policy or your personal data, contact us at contact@learn-urdu.online.
