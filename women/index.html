Your questions

Everything you might want to know before you begin.

Who are these lessons for?

Women and girls aged 13 and over, at any level. Whether you're a complete beginner or want to improve, you're welcome. For younger children, see our Urdu for Kids lessons.

Is the teacher really a woman?

Yes. All lessons are taught by Maryam, a qualified female Urdu teacher. Many women prefer this, and we're glad to offer it.

I know absolutely no Urdu. Is that okay?

Completely. Maryam starts from the very first letter and builds up gently. Total beginners are very welcome.

How long is each lesson?

Each lesson is 50 minutes, private and one-on-one. There's also a shorter 30-minute trial lesson so you can meet Maryam first.

How much does it cost?

Your first trial lesson is at a special introductory price, so you can try with no pressure. Full pricing is shown when you book.

What do I need?

Just a device with Zoom and a quiet, comfortable space. Maryam provides all the learning materials.
